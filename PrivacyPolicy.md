# Privacy Policy - Motion Augmentat-inator

**Last updated:** September 28, 2026

Motion Augmentat-inator ("the app") is developed by SoupFork Studios LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything the app does with your camera happens entirely on your device.

## What the app accesses

**Camera.** The app requires camera access to show a live video feed and highlight motion in it. This is the app's core function and cannot work without it.

## What happens to the camera feed

- Every frame is processed live, on your device, to detect motion and draw the on-screen highlight. It is never sent anywhere.
- If you turn on recording (manual or auto-record) or snapshots, the resulting video/image files are written to the app's private storage on your device only.
- These files are never uploaded, transmitted, synced, or shared with SoupFork Studios, any third party, or any server. The app has no network/internet functionality related to your camera feed, recordings, or snapshots at all.
- Recordings do not include audio - the app does not access your microphone.
- These files are deleted automatically if you uninstall the app. There is currently no separate in-app option to browse or delete them individually; they are accessible only through standard Android developer/file tools, and only on your own device.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no account system, no login, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting or tracking of any kind. We have no way to see your camera feed, your recordings, your snapshots, or any other data related to your use of the app, because none of it ever leaves your device.

## Third parties

The app does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect or transmit any data in the first place. It uses only standard Android/CameraX system APIs to access the camera - this is local device functionality, not a third-party data service.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

## Your control over your data

Since all recordings and snapshots stay on your device, you control them the same way you control any other file on your phone: uninstalling the app removes them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes - for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does transmit data - we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before that feature ships.
